# Human Shaped community

This repository exists so that people building human-shaped software
have a place to talk with one another, and the conversation itself lives
in [Discussions](https://github.com/humanshaped/community/discussions).
Bring a question you are stuck on, show an app you are building (even
when it is rough), or tell us about a meetup or a Human-Shaped Hackathon
you want to start.

Each cohort also gets its own private space for the five weeks it runs,
so that people can ask anything they like while they are learning, and a
cohort can choose to open its conversation up afterward.

We treat one another the way the
[Educational Model Spec](https://github.com/bhwilkoff/educational-model-spec)
asks people to treat each other when AI is in the room: the people
decide and do the learning, and we say so when something we share was
made with an AI's help. If something here goes wrong, tell Ben
Wilkoff ([@bhwilkoff](https://github.com/bhwilkoff)).

New here? [humanshaped.org](https://humanshaped.org) is the place to
start.
